FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Dallas homeowners about heating and cooling service.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Response times depend on current demand, your location, and the type of service you need. Submit a request and our team will follow up with the earliest available appointment options. Submitting a request does not guarantee appointment availability.

What HVAC services do you offer?

We handle residential AC repair, heating repair, HVAC installation and replacement, and preventive maintenance. If you're not sure which category fits your situation, choose "Other" on the request form and describe what's happening — we'll help you sort it out.

Do you handle emergency AC repairs?

We service urgent cooling and heating problems. On the request form, select "As Soon As Possible" so our team can prioritize your request. Availability depends on our current schedule, and urgent requests aren't guaranteed same-day service.

Can I request a service appointment online?

Yes. Complete the service request form on this page and our automated assistant will acknowledge your request and gather the details our team needs for scheduling. You can also call us at (214) 555-0199 if you'd rather talk it through.

Which areas around Dallas do you serve?

We currently serve Dallas along with nearby communities including Plano, Richardson, Garland, Irving, and Mesquite. Coverage can vary by ZIP code, so include your ZIP code in your request and we'll confirm availability for your address.

What happens after I submit a request?

Our automated assistant receives your request and may follow up to collect additional details needed for scheduling. After that, our team reviews availability and contacts you to confirm the next steps for your service visit. Your request is not a confirmed appointment until we've spoken with you.
