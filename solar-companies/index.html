A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is my home a good fit for solar?+

Roof condition, shade, orientation, local rules, and electricity use all matter. A site assessment helps establish what’s possible for your home.

Do I need a battery?+

Not always. Storage can help you use solar energy after sunset or provide backup when configured for it. The right choice depends on your goals and local electricity rates.

Will solar power my home during an outage?+

A typical grid-connected solar system shuts down during an outage. Backup requires compatible equipment, usually including a battery and an appropriately designed backup circuit.

What does a solar system cost?+

Costs depend on system size, roof conditions, equipment, and installation requirements. A tailored proposal should explain the full price and any applicable incentives.
